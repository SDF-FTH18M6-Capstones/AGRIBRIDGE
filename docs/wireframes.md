## Screen Notes

### 01 Landing page
- Hero: "Farm Fresh. Direct to You."
- Search and Browse Products button
- Featured products
- How AgriBridge works

### 02 Registration and login
- Login with email and password
- Create account (name, phone, email, password)
- Choose role: Farmer or Buyer

### 03 Farmer dashboard
- Summary cards: products, orders, earnings
- My products with Add Product, Edit and Delete
- Latest order with M-Pesa payment status

### 04 Marketplace
- Search by produce or location
- Category filter: All, Vegetables, Fruits, Grains, Others
- Product cards with price, location and farmer name

### 05 Product details
- Image, price per kg and available quantity
- Quantity selector with live total price
- Add to Cart
- Farmer information

### 06 Shopping cart and checkout
- Steps: Review cart, Delivery, Payment, Confirm
- Order summary with delivery fee and total
- Payment: M-Pesa or Pay on delivery

### 07 Buyer dashboard
- Summary cards: total orders and total spending
- Recent orders with delivery and payment status
- Track order: Order placed, Preparing, Out for delivery, Delivered

### 08 Admin dashboard
- Summary cards: users, products, orders
- Manage users, products and orders
- Recent activity
